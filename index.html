Privacy Policy

Forge

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Compose R.A Studio developed Forge as a training log for people who lift weights. It lets the user log workouts set by set, with weight, repetitions, time, set type, and effort (RPE or RIR); use a rest timer and supersets; create routines and custom exercises; follow their strength progress and personal records; see their weekly volume and an estimate of how recovered each muscle is on a muscle map; track body weight, body fat, body measurements, and progress photos; export spreadsheets; and make backups.

All the information entered in the application is stored only on the user's device. The application does not have its own server (backend), does not require creating an account, does not ask for user registration, and does not use any authentication method, such as logging in with Google or any other provider. Its training and body tracking features work without an internet connection.

To remain free of charge, the application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices about advertising where the law requires it. These services never receive the workouts, body data, or photos recorded in the application. The application does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own.

Information privacy and security are a priority for Compose R.A Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, where it is kept, and what choices and rights the user has.

Information Stored on the Device

The information created by the user, including, but not limited to:

  • Workouts: name, date, start and end time, notes, exercises, and each set's weight, repetitions, duration, set type, effort (RPE or RIR), rest time, and supersets
  • Routines: name, notes, and their exercises with their order, target sets and repetitions, rest time, and supersets
  • Custom exercises: name, equipment, primary and secondary muscles, default rest time, and notes
  • Records and statistics: personal records, estimated one-rep maximums, weekly volume, and muscle recovery estimates, all calculated on the device from the logged workouts
  • Body weight: date, weight, optional body fat percentage, notes, and goal weight
  • Body measurements: date and measurements such as neck, shoulders, chest, waist, abdomen, hips, arms, forearms, thighs, and calves
  • Progress photos: the image, its pose (front, side, back, or other), and its date
  • Settings: weight and length units, language, theme, weekly goal, first day of the week, rest timer and effort preferences, one-rep maximum formula, the figure used for the muscle map (male or female), automatic backup preferences, and app lock options

is stored exclusively in the application's private storage on the user's device. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, or have access to this information.

Body weight, body fat, body measurements, and progress photos may be considered health and fitness information. They never leave the device unless the user decides to export them, back them up, or share them, are never sent to advertisers, and are never used to choose advertisements.

The data remains under the exclusive control of the user, unless they decide to share an export or make a backup using the features available in the application.

To suggest the weight and length units and the first day of the week when the application is set up, it reads the country code of the SIM card and of the mobile network, and the region configured on the phone. This does not use the device's location (GPS), does not require any permission, happens on the device, and is not sent anywhere.

Progress Photos

Progress photos are optional and are added only when the user chooses to:

  • Take a photo: the application opens the device's camera app, which saves the picture into a temporary file of the application. Forge does not request the camera permission and never accesses the camera on its own.
  • Pick from gallery: Android's photo picker gives the application access only to the image the user selects.

The application keeps its own copy of each photo in its private storage, resized and saved again as a JPEG image, so the copy does not carry the original file's metadata, such as the place where it was taken. Progress photos do not appear in the device's gallery and are not available to other applications. They can be protected with the app lock described below.

A photo is deleted when the user deletes it in the application, or when the application's data is cleared.

App Lock and Biometrics

From Settings → Data & privacy → App lock, the user can protect the whole application or only the progress photos with a PIN and, optionally, with the device's biometrics (fingerprint or face, depending on the device):

  • The PIN is never stored: the application keeps only a salted cryptographic hash of it (PBKDF2), on the device.
  • Biometric verification is performed by Android. The application only learns whether the verification succeeded; it never receives or stores fingerprints, face data, or any other biometric information.
  • The lock settings are never included in backup files or in Android's backup.
  • "Hide in recent apps" shows a blank card in the app switcher and blocks screenshots of the application.

For the user's privacy, a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. In that case, the user can clear the application's data from the device settings and restore a backup.

What the Application Does Not Do

Forge:

  • Does not require creating an account
  • Does not ask for the user's name, email, or phone number to use the application
  • Does not use authentication through Google, Facebook, or other services
  • Does not sync the recorded information with its own servers or any other server
  • Does not connect to wearables, smartwatches, gym equipment, Health Connect, or other fitness services
  • Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own
  • Does not access the device's location (GPS), contacts, microphone, or SMS messages
  • Does not access the photo gallery or files beyond the items the user selects
  • Does not send, sell, or share the workouts, body data, or photos recorded by the user with anyone, including advertisers

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services. They may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The application shows full-screen advertisements (interstitials) served by Google AdMob. They never appear when the application opens, during the welcome and setup screens, during the first 24 hours after the application is installed, or while a workout is in progress. They appear only occasionally, after the user has completed several actions, such as finishing a workout, logging body weight or measurements, adding a progress photo, or saving a routine or a custom exercise, with at least two minutes between two advertisements. They are shown at a natural pause, right after an action is completed.

To serve and measure advertisements, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and, where the user allows it, personalize them, AdMob may collect:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information
  • Interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Performance and diagnostic information of the advertising SDK, including crash logs

On devices that support them, AdMob may also use Android's advertising APIs of the Privacy Sandbox (such as Topics and Attribution), which the user can manage or turn off in the device settings.

The workouts, body data, and photos recorded in the application are never shared with AdMob and are not used to choose advertisements. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (Privacy Choices)

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform provided by Google, to request and record privacy choices about advertising as required in each region. These choices are stored on the device and passed to Google's advertising services.

Google Play Billing

The application includes Google Play's billing library so that optional in-app purchases can be offered through Google Play. If a purchase is offered and the user makes it, it is processed entirely by Google Play: payment details are entered in Google Play and are never received by the application or by Compose R.A Studio. Google handles the purchase, its receipt, and any refund according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Sharing Initiated by the User

Some features open other applications. They are used only when the user requests them, and the user can review the content before sending it:

  • Exports (Excel and CSV): from Settings → Data & privacy → Backup & export, the user can export their workouts, sets, records, body weight, and measurements as an Excel file (.xlsx), a CSV file, or a ZIP file with one CSV file per table. Exports do not include photos. The file can be saved in the location the user chooses or shared through the Android share menu.
  • Backup files: a backup can likewise be saved or shared, as described below.
  • Contact and sharing buttons: "Contact support" opens the user's email application with a prepared message that includes the application version, the Android version, and the device model; "Share the app" and "Rate the app" open the share menu or Google Play. Nothing is sent until the user confirms it.

Once information is shared, it is handled by the application or service the user chose, according to its own privacy policy. To share a file, the application creates a temporary copy of it in its private cache, which Android may clear at any time and which is deleted when the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Backups

From Settings → Data & privacy → Backup & export, the user can create a backup file (.forgebackup) with all the information of the application: workouts, routines, custom exercises, body weight and measurements, progress photos, and settings. The lock PIN and the other lock settings are never included. The file is created only when the user requests it and is saved in the location the user chooses, such as the device's storage or a cloud storage service. If that location is synchronized with a cloud storage service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

The user can also turn on automatic backups. The user chooses a folder, and the application then writes a backup file into it periodically, at night and when the battery is not low, keeping only the most recent files. The application can access only the folder the user chose, and automatic backups can be turned off at any time.

Backup files are not encrypted and may contain progress photos, so they should be kept in a safe place. Restoring a backup replaces all the information currently stored in the application; a copy of the previous information is kept on the device so the last restore can be undone.

If backup is enabled in the device's Android settings, Android may also include the training database and the settings of the application in the backup of the user's Google account. Progress photos and the lock settings are not included in that backup. When moving to a new device with a direct device-to-device transfer, Android may also copy the progress photos; the lock settings always stay on the original device. That backup is managed by Google under the user's account, and Compose R.A Studio has no access to it. It can be turned off from the device's backup settings.

Notifications and Rest Alerts

While a workout is in progress, the application shows a notification with the workout name, the elapsed time, and, during a rest, the remaining time and the next set. When a rest ends, it can play a sound and vibrate. These notifications may appear on the lock screen depending on the device's notification settings.

The application can also schedule an exact alarm so the end-of-rest alert arrives on time with the screen off. This uses Android's "Alarms & reminders" special access, which the user grants or revokes from Settings → Rest timer → On-time alert with the screen off or from the device settings.

Notifications can be disabled at any time from the device settings.

Data Security

The information is kept in the application's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications, and benefits from the storage encryption of the device where it is available. The optional app lock adds a PIN or biometric check to the whole application or to the progress photos. To protect the information further, it is recommended to use a screen lock on the device and to keep backup files and exports in a safe place.

Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it. The user can:

  • Edit or delete workouts, routines, custom exercises, body weight entries, measurements, and progress photos from within the application. Some deletions can be undone for a few seconds with "Undo"
  • Delete backup files and exports from the location where they were saved, and turn off automatic backups
  • Delete all the information by clearing the application's data from the device settings or by uninstalling the application

Backups, exports, and other files saved or shared by the user remain wherever the user saved or sent them until the user deletes them. Because Compose R.A Studio does not operate servers or user accounts, it does not hold a copy of the user's information that it could access or delete.

Information collected by Google's services is retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings. For requests about that information, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio, which will help direct them to the right place.
